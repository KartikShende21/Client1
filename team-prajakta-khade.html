Executive Leadership Profile

Mrs. Prajakta Khade

Creative Head & Interior Director • K Arch Architects

Creative Vision & Spatial Philosophy

"Architecture reaches its highest expression when the interior spaces breathe life into human experience. We design environments that balance sensory luxury, biophilic materials, and functional serenity."

Professional Background

As the Creative Head at K Arch Architects, Mrs. Prajakta Khade leads the firm's creative identity, interior architecture, and experiential design practices. Her human-centric approach transforms cold structural volumes into warm, soulful sanctuaries.

She directs the interior aesthetic schemes across luxury high-rise towers, private penthouses, corporate headquarters, and high-end residential clubhouses. Her work seamlessly integrates contemporary materials, bespoke joinery, intelligent illumination, and acoustic balance.

Key Areas of Expertise

  • Grand Double-Height Entrance Lobbies: Creating magnificent arrival experiences with dramatic marble cladding, kinetic chandeliers, and concierge spatial flows.
  • Clubhouse & Podium Amenities: Designing world-class resident amenities including infinity pools, sky lounges, wellness spas, and private theater suites.
  • 3D Spatial Ergonomics: Supervising 3D floor cutaways and isometric spatial zoning ensuring zero wasted space and optimal natural daylight.
  • Biophilic Materiality: Sustainable timbers, natural stone, indoor green atriums, and low-VOC finishes for healthier indoor living.
