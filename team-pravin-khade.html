Professional Vision & Practice

Ar. Pravin Eknath Khade is an acclaimed Mumbai-based architect who established K Arch Architects in 2012. With near two decades of domain mastery navigating the intricate urban fabric of the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR), Ar. Pravin has pioneered transformative high-rise and mixed-use architectural methodologies that successfully balance bold aesthetic ambition with stringent municipal compliance.

Under his visionary guidance, K Arch Architects has delivered iconic multi-storey residential towers, corporate business parks, and large-scale master-planned townships for premier developers such as N. Rose Developers, Swastik Group, Royal Realtors, and Shreeji Sharan Group.

Core Areas of Architectural Mastery

High-Rise Skyscrapers

35 to 60-storey aerodynamic tower design, shear core coordination, seismic load calculations, and wind tunnel optimizations.

Society Redevelopment (FSI)
